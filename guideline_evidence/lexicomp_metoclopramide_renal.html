Metoclopramide (Gavistal) • Clinical Drug Information & FDA Label

Dosage Adjustments: Renal Impairment & Geriatric Safety Profile

FDA WARNING: TARDIVE DYSKINESIA & EXTRA PYRAMIDAL SYMPTOMS (EPS)

Treatment with metoclopramide can cause tardive dyskinesia and acute extrapyramidal reactions, which are often irreversible. The risk increases with duration of treatment, cumulative dose, advanced age (geriatric patients, especially elderly females), and renal impairment (impaired clearance). Avoid treatment for longer than 12 weeks. Discontinue immediately in patients who develop signs or symptoms of movement disorders.

Clinical Pharmacist Recommendation for Ny. Sunarti

1. Inpatient Phase: Karena CrCl = 30.69 mL/menit, dosis Injeksi Metoklopramid seharusnya disesuaikan menjadi 5 mg IV q8h (50% reduksi). Karena muntah telah teratasi sejak 16/10, injeksi metoklopramid dihentikan.
2. Discharge Phase: Resep pulang tertulis Gavistal (Metoklopramid) 10 mg tab 3x1. Ini adalah dosis penuh tanpa penyesuaian ginjal dan harus DIHENTIKAN total.
